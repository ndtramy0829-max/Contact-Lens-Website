Eye Care Tips

Keep your eyes healthy

  • 01
    Never sleep in your lenses

    Give your eyes time to breathe overnight.

  • 02
    Replace on schedule

    Follow the recommended replacement timeline for your lens type.

  • 03
    Keep them clean

    Use only approved solution and never use water or saliva.

  • 04
    Visit your optometrist

    Annual eye exams ensure your prescription stays accurate.
