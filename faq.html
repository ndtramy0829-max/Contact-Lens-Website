Frequently Asked Questions

Do you carry prescription lenses?

Currently, we only carry 0.00 / plano lenses with no vision-correcting power.

What type of lenses do you carry?

All of our lenses are daily disposable lenses designed for single-day wear.

Do you carry monthly, quarterly, or bi-yearly lenses?

No. At this time, we only carry daily lenses. We do not currently offer monthly, quarterly, bi-yearly, or other extended-wear options.

How many lenses are included in the listed price?

Unless otherwise stated, the listed price is for 1 pair (2 lenses) — one lens for each eye.

Are the lenses reusable?

No. Our daily lenses are intended for single-day use only. They should be discarded after wear and should not be reused the following day.

Do you offer pickup?

Yes! Local pickup is available in Orange County (OC) and San Diego (SD). Pickup details will be provided after your order is confirmed.

Do you offer shipping?

Yes! We offer shipping within the United States.

Do you ship internationally?

At this time, we only ship within the U.S. and do not offer international shipping.

Can I order different colors or styles for each eye?

If the styles are available individually, please contact us before ordering and we can let you know what options are available.

How do I choose a color or style?

Please refer to the photos and descriptions provided for each lens. Keep in mind that the final color may look slightly different depending on your natural eye color, lighting, makeup, and screen settings.

Can I sleep in the lenses?

No. Our lenses should be removed before sleeping and discarded after use.

How should I handle my lenses?

Always wash and completely dry your hands before handling contact lenses. Follow proper contact-lens hygiene and the instructions provided with your lenses.

What if I experience discomfort while wearing the lenses?

Remove the lenses immediately if you experience pain, redness, irritation, blurred vision, or unusual discomfort. Do not continue wearing them, and consult an eye-care professional if symptoms persist.

Are colored contacts safe for everyone?

Contact lenses may not be suitable for everyone. We recommend consulting an eye-care professional to make sure contact lenses are appropriate for your eyes and fit properly.
